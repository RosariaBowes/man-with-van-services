About Us

Man with Van Services South Wales is a trusted removals and transport company providing reliable, affordable, and professional moving services based in Cardiff and the wider UK.

Whether you're moving home, relocating your office, transporting furniture, or requiring a single-item collection, we take pride in delivering a friendly and dependable service tailored to your needs. Our experienced team handles every job with care and professionalism, ensuring your belongings arrive safely and on time.

Our Vision

Our goal is simple: to provide a reliable, stress-free service that customers can trust. We are committed to delivering excellent customer care, maintaining high standards of professionalism, and building lasting relationships through honest, dependable work.

Supporting Local Sport

At Man with Van Services South Wales, we believe in giving back to the local community. We are proud sponsors of a local football team, helping to support grassroots sport and provide opportunities for players to develop and enjoy the game.

Community involvement is an important part of our values, and we are delighted to see the team representing the area while wearing kits sponsored by our business.
